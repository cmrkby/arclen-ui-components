# Provenance

Grain Gradient is original Arclen work. The procedural shader, grain mixer, contour descriptors, interpolation, palette math and all three compositions (Original, Flowing ribbons and Flowing blanket) were authored for Arclen. No third-party implementation, shader, texture or image is included.

The core has no runtime dependencies. React is supplied by the consuming application.

The posters are captures of Grain Gradient's own renderer, encoded as lossless WebP. Their hashes are listed in assets/PROVENANCE.md. No third-party attribution or licence notice is required.
