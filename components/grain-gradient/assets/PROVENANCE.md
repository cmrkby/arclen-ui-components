# Poster provenance

All three images are original Arclen procedural artwork, not reference captures. Original warm was rendered from the portable package. Ribbons retains its admitted native Metal phase-zero capture. Flowing blanket was captured from the actual current native Metal renderer at a representative natural bottom-centered phase (recorded precisely in its creation receipt), 1440×600 CSS pixels, then encoded losslessly without crop, resize or color edit. Creation receipts are retained in the local release evidence.
