Tidal Glass opens as a pill button over the Tidal Fold wave. Activate **Look closer** to magnify the scene inside the lens. Click again to return, or use Enter or Space while the button is focused.

In Tweaks, choose **Shape preset** to switch between Pill button, Card and Badge. Each shape has its own width, height and corner radius in pixels. Switching shapes restores that shape’s dimensions. Set a small radius for square corners, or at least half the short side for a pill. The lens fits within the available frame on narrow screens.

The Button and Badge groups each include an editable label. The badge is static. The card displays the caption and optional play mark, and docks near the bottom on phones. Pass the same settings as React props or call the vanilla instance’s `update` method.

Choose Tidal Fold for the seven-second wave loop, or Iris Drift, Salt Meadow or Velodrome for a still. Playback speed ranges from 0.25× to 2×. Supply a same-origin or CORS-enabled backplate through `videoSrc` or `imageSrc`. The host sets the size of the scene.

Refraction and Magnify affect the interior of the lens. Edge Refraction, Bevel Width and Bevel Depth shape the rim. Dispersion separates the colour channels at the edge. Frost softens every lens shape. Button frost adds diffusion and a smoky tint to the Look closer pill. Set both frost controls to zero for clear glass. Reduced motion and pause hold the frame, and the renderer suspends offscreen.
