# Iconsax navigation icons

Full bleed navigation incorporates the inner chevrons from Iconsax's `arrow-circle-left` and `arrow-circle-right` icons. Their enclosing circle is replaced by the component's button surface; colour, framing and stroke rendering are adapted. These two graphics are integrated into the functional navigation markup in `src/core/index.ts`. No standalone Iconsax SVG files or icon pack are included.
